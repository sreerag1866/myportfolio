03 — ABOUT THE STUDIO

A designer's studio practice

Studio SA is the personal art practice of Sreerag Ayyappath, a graphic designer based in Dubai with over ten years in print and brand design. These pieces grew out of the same generative, print-production instincts he brings to client work — halftones, colour fields, plotted lines — reworked as art for its own sake.

Every print is produced locally to order, so nothing sits in a warehouse until you ask for it.
